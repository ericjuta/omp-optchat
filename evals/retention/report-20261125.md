# Retention by chat length, and a 64,000-byte view: results (2026-11-25)

Plan: `prereg-20261125.md` with `prereg-20261125-amendment-1.md`. Machine-readable report: `result-20261125.json`. Raw artifacts stay out of git.

Setup: main model `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5-5`, compressor `anthropic/claude-haiku-5-5`, OMP 19.2.1, synthetic corpus, 3 seeds, 24 questions per cell, one question per turn, memory-only (no System One gates). Observed spend was $16.44 of the $40 cap, including one failed cell ($0.50).

## Reading

- The default view kept every answer the whole-history arm got right at 48 and 128 records. At 512 records it kept 90%.
- The loss is in citations, not in values. At 512 records both view arms found 98.6% of the values whole history found. Of the 9 wrong answers at 128,000 bytes, 6 were graded `fabricated-citation` and 3 were the ambiguous question. At 64,000 bytes, 15 of 18 were `fabricated-citation`. In 16 of those 21 citation failures the model never opened the cited record: it quoted from a summary line.
- Decision rule: the 64,000-byte arm has 9 fewer correct answers than the 128,000-byte arm, more than the 2 allowed. Its main-model cost is 15.6% lower. The 128,000-byte default stands, and the view-size question is closed.
- On this corpus, which fits the main model's context, the view arm cost more than whole history at every length with the default view.

## Limits

- Synthetic corpus, memory-only arms, one main model and one compressor. Nothing here measures real chats, prefetch or `chat_recall`.
- 72 questions per cell from 3 corpora; questions within a corpus are not independent.
- The `fabricated-citation` grades were not hand-checked against the answers.
- The first whole-history cell ran one commit earlier than the rest (`5773a3a` against `c4030c5`); `src/` is the same in both.
- One cell failed before any question and was rerun after amendment 1.
