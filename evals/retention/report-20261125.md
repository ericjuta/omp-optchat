## Reading

- The default view kept every answer the whole-history arm got right at 48 and 128 records. At 512 records it kept 90%.
- The loss is in citations, not in values. At 512 records both view arms found 98.6% of the values whole history found. Of the 9 wrong answers at 128,000 bytes, 6 were graded `fabricated-citation` and 3 were the ambiguous question. At 64,000 bytes, 15 of 18 were `fabricated-citation`. In 16 of those 21 citation failures the model did not open the cited record with `zoom`. See the hand-check below: the quotes are real, so most of this loss is how the runner grades, not model behaviour.
- Decision rule: the 64,000-byte arm has 9 fewer correct answers than the 128,000-byte arm, more than the 2 allowed. Its main-model cost is 15.6% lower. The 128,000-byte default stands, and the view-size question is closed.
- On this corpus, which fits the main model's context, the view arm cost more than whole history at every length with the default view.

## Limits

- Synthetic corpus, memory-only arms, one main model and one compressor. Nothing here measures real chats, prefetch or `chat_recall`.
- 72 questions per cell from 3 corpora; questions within a corpus are not independent.
- The first whole-history cell ran one commit earlier than the rest (`5773a3a` against `c4030c5`); `src/` is the same in both.
- One cell failed before any question and was rerun after amendment 1.
- The artifacts keep only the first 240 characters of each answer and no tool-call names other than `zoom`, so the hand-check below covers the first citation of each answer and cannot name the tool that returned the text.

## Hand-check of the citation grades (2026-11-26, $0)

`bun run evals/retention/handcheck-20261125.ts .verify/retention-20261125/cell-s?-512-on-*000.json` rebuilds each corpus from its seed and compares every complete citation in the stored answer previews with the seeded records.

- All 21 visible quotes are word-for-word text of the record they cite. None is invented. 19 cite a record the question's answer comes from; 2 cite a real distractor record.
- An earlier version of this report said the model "quoted from a summary line". That was wrong: the scorer grades a quote found only in the view as `ungrounded`, not `fabricated-citation`.
- The scorer counts original text only from `zoom` and `work` results (`scripts/evidence/host.ts`). The on arm also has `chat_search` and `chat_evidence`, which return original text and are not counted. Each of the 16 answers without a `zoom` page of the cited record made one tool round before answering. The likely reading is that the model read the text through one of those tools; the artifacts do not record which, so this is not confirmed.
- In the other 5 answers the model did open a source record with `zoom`, and the preview is cut at 240 characters, so the citation that failed is not visible.
- Consequence: the 63/72 and 54/72 strict scores at 512 records understate the view arms by up to 6 and 15 answers. The scores are not regraded here, because the stored previews are incomplete. The view-size comparison is affected the same way and is not regraded either.
